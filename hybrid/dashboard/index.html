      Ranking sources and methodology

      New here? How to read these cards
      • Technical signal: bullish / bearish / no clear signal describes indicator direction—not an order, a profit forecast, or personal financial advice.
      • Signal score runs from 0 (bearish) to 1 (bullish). It is not a probability of making money. Thresholds come from the strategy configuration.
      • Session is the exchange trading day analyzed. Weekends and holidays do not produce a new session. Results use completed daily bars, at least 20 minutes after close.
      • Adjusted close is Yahoo's historical closing price adjusted for corporate actions, not a live quote or guaranteed fill price.
      • This first version uses technical indicators only, not LLM research. Saved results are historical and can differ from today's market.
      • Rank compares company market capitalization within its sector in the saved source snapshot. Rankings are not refreshed automatically, source quotes may be delayed, and US-listed foreign companies/ADRs may be included. A company appears only once, even if it has multiple share classes.
      • Sector labels follow the source classification; a company may operate in several industries. The ten categories here do not include the materials sector. ETF holdings can overlap with the stock watchlist.
      • Enabling research requires the operator to configure STOCK_SHADOW_ENABLED=true with Alpaca calendar access and database storage. It never enables stock trading.
